IN THE COURT OF THE DISTRICT AND SESSIONS JUDGE, KAPURTHALA
Sessions Case No. 142/2023
State of Punjab v. Harjinder Singh

JUDGMENT

1. The accused is charged under Section 307 IPC and Section 25 of the Arms Act for allegedly firing at the complainant on 14.03.2023. The prosecution examined eight witnesses.

2. It is held that the testimony of an injured witness carries great evidentiary weight, as laid down in State of U.P. v. Naresh (2011) 4 SCC 324. The medical evidence corroborates the ocular account of the complainant.

3. The defence argued that the recovery of the pistol under Section 27 of the Evidence Act was not witnessed by any independent person. It is well settled that recovery evidence, though not inadmissible, must be viewed with caution when independent witnesses are available but not joined. Reference is made to AIR 1996 SC 1393.

4. The law is settled that intention to kill under Section 307 IPC may be inferred from the nature of the weapon, the part of the body targeted and the circumstances. The complainant suffered a gunshot injury to the abdomen, which the doctor opined was dangerous to life.

5. Accordingly, the Court holds that the prosecution has proved the charge under Section 307 IPC beyond reasonable doubt. The accused is convicted. The charge under Section 25 of the Arms Act is not proved as sanction was not produced and the accused is acquitted of that charge.

6. The matter is listed for hearing on sentence on 15/12/2026. The accused shall remain in custody till then.
